# Codex Execution Prompt

You are acting as a senior full-stack architect, UI engineer and implementation specialist.

Your task is to build **Phase 8 — Lifestyle Pillar** for the Mula Group ecosystem repository.

## Project context
Mula Group is a modular business ecosystem platform with:
- one main portal: `mulagroup.eu`
- six pillar sites:
  - strategy
  - digital
  - commerce
  - industry
  - projects
  - lifestyle

The Lifestyle pillar is responsible for:
- premium experience design
- brand-driven initiatives
- lifestyle venture concepts
- event and activation concepts
- premium service concepts
- experience architecture
- premium perception and aspirational growth layers
- connecting emotional value with business structure

This site is not a generic event page, creative studio page or luxury moodboard.
It must feel like a **premium experience and brand-venture platform** for ambitious, high-perception initiatives that still require real structure and business logic.

# PHASE 8 — LIFESTYLE PILLAR

This phase is about building the main Lifestyle pillar website:
`lifestyle.mulagroup.eu`

---

## Main goal
Build the Lifestyle pillar homepage / core pillar page using the shared foundation and the Lifestyle blueprint.

The page must:
- position the Lifestyle pillar as the premium experience and perception layer of the ecosystem,
- explain how experience, brand value and structured growth can work together,
- show premium and aspirational capabilities without becoming fluffy or decorative,
- guide users toward the right entry point for premium initiatives,
- establish Mula Lifestyle as a high-trust partner for refined, experience-led ventures.

The result should feel:
- premium
- elegant
- aspirational
- structured
- refined
- calm
- business-aware rather than purely aesthetic

---

## Scope of work
Build the main Lifestyle pillar page with the following sections:

### 1. Header / navigation
Create a pillar-specific navigation aligned with the ecosystem style.

Suggested navigation:
- Overview
- Services
- Experience Types
- Process
- Contact

Include one primary CTA such as:
- `Discuss a premium project`

Navigation must feel connected to the shared Mula Group system.

---

### 2. Hero section
Build a Lifestyle hero section that communicates:
- premium experiences
- brand-driven ventures
- lifestyle concepts with structure
- refined growth through perception, quality and experience

Include:
- label / eyebrow
- headline
- subheadline
- 1–2 CTAs
- visual treatment aligned with the premium corporate-tech system

The hero should feel elegant and aspirational, but not flashy, loud or luxury-cliché.

---

### 3. Trust / intro section
Build a section that explains:
- why this is more than aesthetics or event styling,
- why premium perception must connect to structure and business logic,
- why experience can become part of a growth system,
- why the Lifestyle pillar exists inside the wider ecosystem.

This section should reduce ambiguity and establish credibility.

---

### 4. Services section
Build a premium services section for the Lifestyle pillar.

It should include the core service areas from the Lifestyle blueprint, such as:
- Premium Venture Concepts
- Event & Experience Design
- Brand-Driven Initiatives
- Lifestyle Project Positioning
- Experience Architecture
- Integrated Experience Systems

Each service block should:
- be concise,
- be premium but clear,
- remain business-relevant,
- avoid becoming abstract or decorative.

---

### 5. Experience types section
Build a dedicated section showing the types of premium and lifestyle initiatives the pillar supports.

This section may include:
- premium services
- events and activations
- hospitality experiences
- brand-led concepts
- lifestyle ventures
- audience-facing premium initiatives

The section should show that different premium initiatives require different experience models, audience logic and growth structures.

Do not make this section look like a fashion/luxury editorial collage.

---

### 6. Who it’s for section
Build a section that helps users self-identify.

Use cases may include:
- premium brands
- event and activation concepts
- hospitality / lifestyle initiatives
- entrepreneurs building aspirational ventures
- multi-pillar projects requiring a strong experience layer

This section should help the right user feel recognized quickly.

---

### 7. Process section
Build the Lifestyle process section.

Suggested structure:
- Understand
- Define
- Design
- Refine
- Expand

This section should communicate that Mula Lifestyle works through structured experience development, not loose creative moodboarding.

---

### 8. Offer formats section
Build a section presenting practical engagement formats, such as:
- Lifestyle Concept Session
- Event or Experience Blueprint
- Brand Experience Structure
- Premium Venture Design
- Integrated Experience System

This section should make it easy to understand how collaboration can start in a realistic way.

---

### 9. Differentiators section
Build a section showing why Mula Lifestyle is different.

This should communicate:
- experience connected to business logic
- premium perception with real structure
- ability to connect lifestyle concepts with Digital, Commerce, Projects and Strategy
- support for both standalone concepts and larger ecosystem initiatives
- refined work that still leads toward execution

Do not make this sound like generic luxury branding copy.

---

### 10. Cross-pillar logic section
Build a section showing how Lifestyle connects into the wider Mula Group ecosystem.

This section should explain that Lifestyle often connects with:
- Projects
- Digital
- Commerce
- Strategy

The visual and content logic should make the ecosystem feel refined, connected and growth-oriented.

---

### 11. FAQ section
Build an FAQ section using the Lifestyle blueprint logic.

It should handle common questions such as:
- whether this is only for events or hospitality
- whether early-stage premium ideas are supported
- whether Lifestyle is more about branding or business
- whether premium concepts can connect to sales and digital systems
- how Lifestyle supports larger projects

---

### 12. Final CTA / contact section
Build a clear final CTA section that invites:
- discussing a premium project
- sending a concept
- starting with a stronger experience and lifestyle direction

A clean placeholder or basic form shell is enough if deeper backend logic is not part of this phase.

---

### 13. Footer
Use the shared ecosystem footer logic aligned with the Lifestyle pillar context.
