# Codex Execution Prompt

You are acting as a senior full-stack architect, UI engineer and implementation specialist.

Your task is to build **Phase 6 — Industry Pillar** for the Mula Group ecosystem repository.

## Project context
Mula Group is a modular business ecosystem platform with:
- one main portal: `mulagroup.eu`
- six pillar sites:
  - strategy
  - digital
  - commerce
  - industry
  - projects
  - lifestyle

The Industry pillar is responsible for:
- technical capability
- machinery and equipment support
- diagnostics and problem solving
- service structure
- operational process support
- real-world execution depth
- connecting technical operations with strategy, sales and digital systems

This site is not a generic workshop or machinery-service page.
It must feel like a **premium industrial capability platform** for businesses that need technical credibility, operational structure and implementation strength.

## Main goal
Build the Industry pillar homepage / core pillar page using the shared foundation and the Industry blueprint.

The page must:
- position the Industry pillar as real technical and operational capability,
- explain machinery, service, diagnostics and process support as structured business value,
- show practical industrial and technical capabilities,
- guide users toward the right technical / operational entry point,
- establish Mula Industry as a high-trust operational partner.

The result should feel:
- premium
- technical
- structured
- credible
- calm
- implementation-first
- business-relevant rather than workshop-like

---

## Scope of work
Build the main Industry pillar page with the following sections:

### 1. Header / navigation
Create a pillar-specific navigation aligned with the ecosystem style.

Suggested navigation:
- Overview
- Services
- Capabilities
- Process
- Contact

Include one primary CTA such as:
- `Book a technical consultation`

Navigation must feel connected to the shared Mula Group system.

---

### 2. Hero section
Build an Industry hero section that communicates:
- industrial capability
- technical support
- machinery and service structure
- operational strength in real business contexts

Include:
- label / eyebrow
- headline
- subheadline
- 1–2 CTAs
- visual treatment aligned with the premium corporate-tech system

The hero should feel strong and credible, but not heavy, dated or workshop-like.

---

### 3. Trust / intro section
Build a section that explains:
- why this is more than reactive service,
- why technical capability matters as part of broader business performance,
- why operations, service and machinery logic should be structured,
- why Industry strengthens the wider Mula Group ecosystem.

This section should reduce ambiguity and establish practical trust.

---

### 4. Services section
Build a premium services section for the Industry pillar.

It should include the core service areas from the Industry blueprint, such as:
- Technical Advisory
- Machinery Support
- Diagnostics & Problem Solving
- Service Structure Design
- Operational Process Support
- Technical-Commerce Integration

Each service block should:
- be concise,
- be practical,
- be credible for technical audiences,
- remain business-readable.

---

### 5. Capabilities section
Build a dedicated section showing the main technical and industrial capability areas.

This section may include:
- machinery support
- diagnostics
- technical evaluation
- service logic
- process improvement
- operational integration

The section should show that Mula Industry supports both immediate problems and stronger operational structures.

Do not make this section look like a spare-parts catalog or workshop services list.

---

### 6. Who it’s for section
Build a section that helps users self-identify.

Use cases may include:
- industrial and technical businesses
- service-based organizations
- equipment-heavy operations
- trade businesses with technical products
- multi-pillar projects needing operational backbone

This section should help the right user feel recognized quickly.

---

### 7. Process section
Build the Industry process section.

Suggested structure:
- Review
- Diagnose
- Structure
- Support
- Strengthen

This section should communicate that Mula Industry works through method and operational logic, not just reactive intervention.

---

### 8. Offer formats section
Build a section presenting practical engagement formats, such as:
- Technical Review
- Machinery Support Session
- Service Process Blueprint
- Equipment & Value Assessment
- Industrial Growth Support

This section should make it easy to understand how collaboration can start.

---

### 9. Differentiators section
Build a section showing why Mula Industry is different.

This should communicate:
- technical support connected to business structure
- real machinery / diagnostics / service understanding
- ability to connect technical work with commerce and digital systems
- stronger operational thinking than typical reactive support
- real-world execution depth

Do not make this sound like generic industrial marketing copy.

---

### 10. Cross-pillar logic section
Build a section showing how Industry connects into the wider Mula Group ecosystem.

This section should explain that Industry often supports or is supported by:
- Strategy
- Commerce
- Digital
- Projects

The visual and content logic should make the ecosystem feel connected and practical.

---

### 11. FAQ section
Build an FAQ section using the Industry blueprint logic.

It should handle common questions such as:
- whether this is only service support
- whether machinery-related business problems are supported
- whether this is only for industrial companies
- whether Industry can connect to sales or digital systems
- whether operational problems beyond purely technical issues are included

---

### 12. Final CTA / contact section
Build a clear final CTA section that invites:
- booking a technical consultation
- sending an operational challenge
- starting with a more structured technical and operational model

A clean placeholder or basic form shell is enough if deeper backend logic is not part of this phase.

---

### 13. Footer
Use the shared ecosystem footer logic aligned with the Industry pillar context.
