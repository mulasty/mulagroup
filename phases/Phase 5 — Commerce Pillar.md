# Codex Execution Prompt

You are acting as a senior full-stack architect, UI engineer and implementation specialist.

Your task is to build **Phase 5 — Commerce Pillar** for the Mula Group ecosystem repository.

## Project context
Mula Group is a modular business ecosystem platform with:
- one main portal: `mulagroup.eu`
- six pillar sites:
  - strategy
  - digital
  - commerce
  - industry
  - projects
  - lifestyle

The Commerce pillar is responsible for:
- sales system design
- marketplace growth
- revenue architecture
- distribution models
- product positioning
- go-to-market structure
- commercial scaling

This site is not a generic e-commerce agency page.
It must feel like a **premium commercial growth platform** for businesses that need structured revenue logic, channel architecture and scalable sales systems.

## Main goal
Build the Commerce pillar homepage / core pillar page using the shared foundation and the Commerce blueprint.

The page must:
- position the Commerce pillar as a system for revenue growth,
- explain sales channels, marketplaces and distribution as structured business architecture,
- show practical commercial capabilities,
- guide users toward the right commercial entry point,
- establish Mula Commerce as a high-trust growth partner.

The result should feel:
- premium
- structured
- commercially intelligent
- business-first
- modern
- high-trust
- oriented around growth systems, not scattered sales tactics

---

## Scope of work
Build the main Commerce pillar page with the following sections:

### 1. Header / navigation
Create a pillar-specific navigation aligned with the ecosystem style.

Suggested navigation:
- Overview
- Services
- Channels
- Process
- Contact

Include one primary CTA such as:
- `Book a commerce consultation`

Navigation must feel connected to the shared Mula Group system.

---

### 2. Hero section
Build a Commerce hero section that communicates:
- revenue systems
- structured sales channels
- marketplaces and distribution
- commercial growth architecture

Include:
- label / eyebrow
- headline
- subheadline
- 1–2 CTAs
- visual treatment aligned with the premium corporate-tech system

The hero should feel commercially strong, but not aggressive or “salesy” in a cheap way.

---

### 3. Trust / intro section
Build a section that explains:
- why Commerce is more than online selling,
- why channels must connect to business logic,
- why marketplaces and sales systems should be structured rather than improvised,
- why monetization should be treated as architecture, not isolated activity.

This section should reduce ambiguity and establish commercial seriousness.

---

### 4. Services section
Build a premium services section for the Commerce pillar.

It should include the core service areas from the Commerce blueprint, such as:
- Commerce Strategy
- Marketplace Entry & Growth
- Sales Channel Design
- Distribution Models
- Product Positioning
- Commercial Growth Systems

Each service block should:
- be concise,
- be practical,
- be revenue-relevant,
- be easy to scan and compare.

---

### 5. Channels section
Build a dedicated section showing the kinds of sales channels and commercial routes the pillar supports.

This section may include:
- Amazon
- Allegro
- eBay
- Own e-commerce
- B2B distribution
- Export / international channels

The section should explain that the right mix depends on product, capacity, market and growth model.

Do not make this section look like a marketplace directory.  
It should feel strategic and commercial.

---

### 6. Who it’s for section
Build a section that helps users self-identify.

Use cases may include:
- product-based businesses
- manufacturers and distributors
- companies entering marketplaces
- brands needing stronger revenue systems
- projects building a go-to-market model

This section should help the right user feel recognized quickly.

---

### 7. Process section
Build the Commerce process section.

Suggested structure:
- Assess
- Position
- Design
- Activate
- Scale

This section should communicate that Mula Commerce works through structured commercial architecture, not fragmented sales actions.

---

### 8. Offer formats section
Build a section presenting practical engagement formats, such as:
- Commerce Audit
- Marketplace Launch Plan
- Sales Channel Blueprint
- Product Positioning Sprint
- Commercial Growth System

This section should make it easy to understand how collaboration can start.

---

### 9. Differentiators section
Build a section showing why Mula Commerce is different.

This should communicate:
- sales systems over isolated listings
- commercial logic over platform hacks
- revenue structure aligned with the wider business model
- connection to Strategy, Digital and Industry
- scalable thinking rather than one-off execution

Do not make this sound like generic marketplace-agency copy.

---

### 10. Cross-pillar logic section
Build a section showing how Commerce connects into the wider Mula Group ecosystem.

This section should explain that Commerce often supports or is supported by:
- Strategy
- Digital
- Industry
- Projects

The visual and content logic should make the ecosystem feel connected and scalable.

---

### 11. FAQ section
Build an FAQ section using the Commerce blueprint logic.

It should handle common questions such as:
- whether this is only for marketplaces
- whether scaling existing sales is supported
- whether this is only for e-commerce brands
- whether commerce can be connected to digital and strategy
- whether international sales and export pathways are included

---

### 12. Final CTA / contact section
Build a clear final CTA section that invites:
- booking a commerce consultation
- sending a commercial challenge
- starting with a more strategic revenue model

A clean placeholder or basic form shell is enough if deeper backend logic is not part of this phase.

---

### 13. Footer
Use the shared ecosystem footer logic aligned with the Commerce pillar context.

---

1. that Commerce is about structured revenue systems, not just “selling online”
2. which commercial problems this pillar solves
3. what services and engagement formats exist
4. how the Commerce pillar works
5. what the best next step is

The page should feel like:
- a premium commerce-growth platform
not:
- a generic agency for online sales setup
