`digital.mulagroup.eu`

# Codex Execution Prompt

You are acting as a senior full-stack architect, UI engineer and implementation specialist.

Your task is to build **Phase 4 — Digital Pillar** for the Mula Group ecosystem repository.

## Project context
Mula Group is a modular business ecosystem platform with:
- one main portal: `mulagroup.eu`
- six pillar sites:
  - strategy
  - digital
  - commerce
  - industry
  - projects
  - lifestyle

The Digital pillar is responsible for:
- digital infrastructure
- websites and platforms
- AI implementation for business
- automation workflows
- CRM and process integration
- connected systems for growth and execution

This site is not a generic software-house page.
It must feel like a **premium digital transformation platform** for businesses that need structure, AI and real implementation.

## Main goal
Build the Digital pillar homepage / core pillar page using the shared foundation and the Digital blueprint.

The page must:
- position the Digital pillar as infrastructure for business growth,
- explain websites, AI and automation as one connected system,
- show practical digital capabilities,
- guide the user toward the right digital entry point,
- establish Mula Digital as a high-trust implementation partner.

The result should feel:
- premium
- structured
- modern
- intelligent
- operationally useful
- business-first, not tool-first

---

## Scope of work
Build the main Digital pillar page with the following sections:

### 1. Header / navigation
Create a pillar-specific navigation aligned with the ecosystem style.

Suggested navigation:
- Overview
- Services
- AI & Automation
- Process
- Contact

Include one primary CTA such as:
- `Book a digital consultation`

Navigation must feel connected to the shared Mula Group system.

---

### 2. Hero section
Build a Digital hero section that communicates:
- digital infrastructure,
- AI for business,
- websites and workflows,
- connected systems for modern growth.

Include:
- label / eyebrow
- headline
- subheadline
- 1–2 CTAs
- visual treatment aligned with premium corporate-tech system

The hero should feel modern and strong, but not noisy or hype-driven.

---

### 3. Trust / intro section
Build a section that explains:
- why this is more than web design,
- why digital systems must connect to business logic,
- why AI and automation are valuable only when they support real operations.

This section should reduce ambiguity and position the pillar as practical and serious.

---

### 4. Services section
Build a premium services section for the Digital pillar.

It should include the core service areas from the Digital blueprint, such as:
- Digital Strategy
- Website & Platform Architecture
- AI Implementation for Business
- Automation Workflows
- CRM & Process Integration
- Digital Growth Systems

Each service block should:
- be concise,
- business-relevant,
- implementation-oriented,
- easy to scan.

---

### 5. AI & Automation section
Build a dedicated section showing how AI and automation are used in practical business settings.

This section should explain things like:
- AI assistants
- lead qualification
- content workflows
- CRM automation
- internal process support
- decision support systems

The section must avoid AI hype and instead focus on usefulness, structure and business value.

---

### 6. Who it’s for section
Build a section that helps users self-identify.

Use cases may include:
- growing businesses
- traditional companies in digital transition
- premium brands needing stronger online infrastructure
- teams implementing AI
- multi-pillar projects requiring a digital layer

This section should help the right user feel “this is for us”.

---

### 7. Process section
Build the Digital process section.

Suggested structure:
- Assess
- Structure
- Integrate
- Implement
- Improve

This section should communicate that Digital is built around system design and execution, not random output production.

---

### 8. Offer formats section
Build a section presenting practical engagement formats, such as:
- Digital Audit
- Website Architecture Sprint
- AI Implementation Plan
- Automation Setup
- Digital Growth System

This section should help users understand how the work can begin in a realistic way.

---

### 9. Differentiators section
Build a section showing why Mula Digital is different.

This should communicate:
- websites as systems, not isolated pages
- AI as infrastructure, not hype
- business-first digital architecture
- connection to Strategy, Commerce and Projects
- implementation-aware thinking

Do not make this sound like generic agency copy.

---

### 10. Cross-pillar logic section
Build a section showing how Digital connects into the wider Mula Group ecosystem.

This section should explain that Digital often supports:
- Strategy
- Commerce
- Projects
- Lifestyle
and sometimes Industry

The visual and content logic should make the ecosystem feel connected and scalable.

---

### 11. FAQ section
Build an FAQ section using the Digital blueprint logic.

It should handle common questions such as:
- whether this is only web development
- whether AI can be implemented in an existing business
- whether CRM is required first
- whether Digital can be connected to Strategy
- whether the pillar supports broader systems, not only one page

---

### 12. Final CTA / contact section
Build a clear final CTA section that invites:
- booking a digital consultation
- sending a digital challenge
- starting with digital clarity and practical implementation

A clean placeholder or basic form shell is enough if deeper backend logic is not part of this phase.

---

### 13. Footer
Use the shared ecosystem footer logic aligned with the Digital pillar context.
